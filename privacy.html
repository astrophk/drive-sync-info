Privacy policy

rclone drive sync is a personal tool used only by its owner.

  • It accesses Google Drive only to copy files between the owner's computer and the owner's own Drive.
  • No data is collected, stored on any server, shared with or sold to anyone. Files move only between the owner's computer and Google Drive.
  • Google user data is used solely for this sync and is not used for advertising or any other purpose.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
